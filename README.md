# Slop New York

This edition starts on foot with the supplied civilian character. Choose Spider-Man or the Flash at the portrait. Q toggles the selected suit. The Flash runs at 10× civilian speed with a lightning trail. As Spider-Man, hold R near a building to swing and release to carry your momentum forward. On foot, S turns the character back toward the camera; E takes a nearby slow-moving street car or lets you out once it slows down. Click the scene for mouse look and press Esc to free the cursor. The same character can walk, ride the bicycle, drive the taxi, or pilot the helicopter and Spinner. See [character credits](spider-assets/NOTICE.txt).

A New York City simulation built with [three.js](https://threejs.org/), WebGPURenderer, and TSL. Explore Manhattan on foot, by car, by bicycle, or by helicopter.

[Try it live](https://mrdoob.github.io/slop-newyork/)

## License and data

This edition adds the Police Spinner from Gregg Tavares' [Cyber Web City](https://github.com/greggman/cyber-web-city). Press **F** or select **Spinner** to pilot it. W/S set cruise speed, A/D steer, Space to climb, and C to descend. Press 2 to return to the yellow cab. The model's [MIT license](skycar-assets/LICENSE-Cyber-Web-City.md) accompanies the meshes.

Select **Style** or press **J** to switch between Realistic and Anime while you play. Anime draws on the released Japanese Seaside Street v10 scene, including its sky gradient, clouds, matte street materials, lighting, and selective slate outlines.
